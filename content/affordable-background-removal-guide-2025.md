## **Best Practices for Cost-Effective Background Removal**

### **Image Preparation Tips**

**Optimize Before Processing:**

- Use high-contrast backgrounds when possible
- Ensure good lighting on your subject
- Avoid cluttered or complex backgrounds
- Shoot at higher resolutions for better AI detection

**Batch Processing Strategy:**

- Group similar images together
- Process during off-peak hours for faster results
- Use consistent naming conventions for organization
- Plan your editing workflow to minimize revisions

### **Quality Control on a Budget**

**Pre-Processing Checklist:**

- ✅ Subject is well-lit and in focus
- ✅ Clear separation between subject and background
- ✅ Image resolution is appropriate for end use
- ✅ File format is supported by your chosen tool

**Post-Processing Review:**

- ✅ Edge quality meets your standards
- ✅ No unwanted artifacts or remnants
- ✅ Transparent areas are clean
- ✅ Image resolution is preserved
