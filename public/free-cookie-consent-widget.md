## **Why Do You Need a Cookie Consent Widget?**

If your website uses cookies to track users, you must inform them and obtain their consent.  
A **cookie consent widget** ensures you stay compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations.

It also builds trust with your visitors by showing that you care about their privacy and data security.  
By displaying a simple and customizable consent banner, you can stay transparent and avoid legal issues.

---

## **How to Add a Cookie Consent Widget (Step-by-Step Guide)**

### **1. Customize the Cookie Banner**

Choose your banner style, message, and button text.  
For example, you can write:  
_"We use cookies to improve your browsing experience. By continuing, you agree to our cookie policy."_

### **2. Copy the Embed Script**

After customizing, you will get a code snippet like this:

```html
<script src="https://yourwidgetcdn.com/cookie-consent.js" async></script>
```

### **3. Add the Script to Your Website**

Paste the script into your site just before the closing `</body>` tag.  
Once added, your cookie banner will automatically appear for all new visitors.
