## **Why Add a Feedback Form to Your Website?**

A **feedback form** allows you to directly connect with your audience and understand their needs, opinions, and pain points.  
By collecting feedback, you can improve your products, services, and overall website experience.

With a feedback form, visitors can easily share their suggestions, complaints, or appreciation without having to contact you by email.  
This makes it easier to gather insights and build stronger relationships with your audience.

---

## **How to Add a Feedback Form (Step-by-Step Guide)**

### **1. Customize Your Form**

You can create a feedback form with fields such as name, email, rating, and comments.  
The design should match your website theme, and you can include a simple question like:  
_"How can we improve your experience on our website?"_

### **2. Copy the Embed Script**

After customizing your form, you will receive an embed script like the one below:

```html
<script src="https://yourwidgetcdn.com/feedback-form.js" async></script>
```

### **3. Add the Code to Your Website**

Place this code before the closing `</body>` tag on your website.  
Once added, the feedback form will appear instantly, ready for your visitors to use.
