## **Why Add a Review Form to Your Website?**

A **review form** allows your customers to leave ratings and testimonials directly on your site.  
Positive reviews build trust, improve credibility, and influence potential buyers’ decisions.

By showcasing real customer experiences, you increase the chances of new visitors becoming paying customers.  
It is one of the most powerful forms of social proof that boosts sales and loyalty.

---

## **How to Add a Review Form (Step-by-Step Guide)**

### **1. Customize Your Review Form**

Choose the fields you want, such as star ratings, name, comments, or photos.  
Make sure the form looks professional and matches your website style.

### **2. Copy the Embed Script**

After customizing, you will get an embed code like this:

```html
<script src="https://yourwidgetcdn.com/review-form.js" async></script>
```

### **3. Add It to Your Website**

Paste the script before the closing `</body>` tag.  
Your visitors will now be able to leave reviews directly on your website.
