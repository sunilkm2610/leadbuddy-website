## **Why Announcements Are Important for Your Website?**

An **announcement bar or widget** is one of the simplest but most effective tools for engaging your visitors.  
It helps you **showcase new features, special offers, sales, events, or discounts** instantly at the top or bottom of your website.

Instead of relying only on popups or emails, announcements ensure that every visitor sees your important update in real-time without disturbing their browsing experience.

✅ **Highlight new product launches**  
✅ **Show discounts & limited-time deals**  
✅ **Promote events or webinars**  
✅ **Boost user engagement & sales**

---

## **How to Add Free Announcement to Your Website? (Step-by-Step Guide)**

You don’t need coding skills or a developer. Follow this **simple setup guide**:

### **1. Customize the Theme & Text**

- Choose your widget design: top bar, bottom bar, or popup style.
- Edit the background color, font style, and button text to match your website branding.
- Add your announcement message (example: _“🎉 20% OFF on all products this week!”_).

---

### **2. Copy the Embed Script**

Once you are happy with the design, the tool will generate a **JavaScript embed code**.

```html
<script
  src="https://widget.tryleadbuddy.com/announcement-bar/announcement-bar.js"
  strategy="afterInteractive"
  data-subscription-id="id"
  data-text="🔥 Get 50% off today only!"
  data-button-text="Grab Deal"
  data-button-link="https://example.com"
  data-bg-color="#fef3c7"
  data-text-color="#92400e"
  data-button-color="#facc15"
  data-button-text-color="#000000"
  data-padding="10px"
  data-text-align="center"
  data-container-z-index="9999"
  data-container-position="relative"
  data-width="100%"
  data-button-padding="6px 12px"
  data-button-margin-left="10px"
  data-button-border-radius="4px"
></script>
```

---

### **3. Paste the Code into Your Website**

- Open your website’s **HTML file** or CMS editor (WordPress, Shopify, Next.js, etc.).
- Paste the script **before the closing `</body>` tag**.
- Save & publish your website.

That’s it! 🎉 Your free announcement is now live on your website.
