## **Why Use an Exit Intent Popup?**

An **exit intent popup** detects when a visitor is about to leave your website and displays a targeted message or offer.  
This is a proven way to reduce bounce rates, recover abandoned carts, and increase conversions.

For example, you can offer a discount code, show a free resource, or invite the visitor to join your newsletter right before they leave.  
This last interaction often turns leaving visitors into valuable leads or customers.

---

## **How to Add an Exit Intent Popup (Step-by-Step Guide)**

### **1. Create Your Popup Message**

Decide what you want to offer your visitors before they leave.  
Examples include: _“Wait! Get 10% off your first order”_ or _“Don’t miss our free guide before you go.”_

### **2. Copy the Embed Script**

After setting it up, you will get a script like this:

```html
<script
  src="https://widget.tryleadbuddy.com/exit-popup/exit-popup.js"
  strategy="afterInteractive"
  data-subscription-id="f9458b5f-42af-4ffa-b62b-8d408a8305ed"
  data-title="Wait! Before you go..."
  data-description="Are you sure you want to leave? Subscribe to our newsletter for updates and special offers."
  data-placeholder="Enter your email"
  data-button-text="Subscribe"
  data-bg-color="#ffffff"
  data-text-color="#000000"
  data-input-bg-color="#f9f9f9"
  data-input-text-color="#000000"
  data-button-bg-color="#2563eb"
  data-button-text-color="#ffffff"
  data-border-radius="10px"
  data-local-storage-key="exist_popup_shown"
  data-success-message="Thank you for subscribing!"
  data-error-message="Please enter a valid email address."
  data-max-width="380px"
  data-min-width="280px"
  data-max-height="auto"
  data-min-height="auto"
  data-show-once="true"
></script>
```

### **3. Add It to Your Website**

Paste this script into your website before the closing `</body>` tag.  
The popup will automatically trigger whenever a user tries to leave your site.
