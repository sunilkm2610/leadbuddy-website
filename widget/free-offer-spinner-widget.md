## **Why Add an Offer Spinner to Your Website?**

An **offer spinner** is a fun and interactive way to grab your visitors’ attention.  
It allows users to spin a digital wheel to unlock discounts, free products, or special offers.

Gamifying your promotions encourages visitors to engage with your website, stay longer, and convert at a higher rate.  
It is perfect for e-commerce websites, online campaigns, and seasonal promotions.

---

## **How to Add an Offer Spinner (Step-by-Step Guide)**

### **1. Customize the Spin Wheel**

Decide the rewards you want to include on the wheel, such as “10% OFF”, “Free Shipping”, or “Buy 1 Get 1 Free.”  
Design the wheel with colors that match your brand and add engaging call-to-action text.

### **2. Copy the Embed Script**

After setting up, you will receive a code snippet like this:

```html
<script
  src="https://widget.tryleadbuddy.com/offer-spinner/offer-spinner.js"
  strategy="afterInteractive"
  data-subscription-id="f9458b5f-42af-4ffa-b62b-8d408a8305ed"
  data-title="Special Offer Wheel"
  data-offers="10% Off,Free Shipping,20% Off,$5 Coupon,Free Gift,15% Off"
  data-result-for-offers="Use code SPIN1 for 10% Off,Use code: FREESHIP,Use code: 20OFF,Use code: $5COUPON,Use code: GIFT,Use code: 15OFF"
  data-auto-close-after-result="true"
  data-spin-button-text="🎯 Spin Now!"
  data-result-text="Congratulations! You got:"
  data-spinner-size="240"
  data-spin-duration="2500"
  data-auto-close-duration="3000"
  data-colors="#fbbf24,#60a5fa,#34d399,#f87171,#a78bfa,#f472b6,#facc15,#38bdf8"
></script>
```

### **3. Add the Spinner to Your Website**

Insert the script before the closing `</body>` tag.  
Once done, the interactive spin wheel will be live on your site.
